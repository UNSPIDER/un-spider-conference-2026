Panel 2. Earth observation as a regional public good: governance, cooperation and investment专题讨论二：地球观测的区域公共价值——治理、合作与投入

Disasters, river basins and climate risks cross national borders, but Earth observation systems, data policies and institutional responsibilities often remain nationally focused. This panel explores how satellite data and shared geospatial services can be developed and governed as regional public goods that support collective resilience.灾害、流域和气候风险往往超越国界，而地球观测系统、数据政策和机构职责通常仍以国家为单位。本场专题将探讨如何建设和治理卫星数据、共享地理空间服务等区域公共产品，以期增强区域共同韧性。

Session overview会议简介

Drawing on experience in transboundary cooperation, European public data systems and development finance, the panellists will examine the agreements, standards and investment models needed to share data across countries and institutions. The discussion will consider how the costs, responsibilities and benefits of regional data infrastructure should be distributed, particularly when countries have different technical and financial capacities. It will also ask what roles development banks, international organizations and UN-SPIDER should play in financing, coordinating and sustaining trusted cross-border data systems. At the centre of the discussion is a difficult question: who should pay for and maintain shared Earth observation infrastructure when its benefits extend across borders but are not shared equally?结合跨境合作、欧洲公共数据系统和发展融资经验，嘉宾将讨论跨国家和机构共享数据所需的协议、标准与投资模式。讨论将关注区域数据基础设施的成本、责任和收益应如何分配，特别是在各国技术和资金能力存在差异的情况下；并探讨开发银行、国际组织和UN-SPIDER在可信跨境数据系统的融资、协调和持续运行中应发挥何种作用。核心问题是：当地球观测共享基础设施的效益跨越国界但分配并不均等时，应由谁出资并负责维护？

Panel 4. Different landscapes, different risks: adapting EO for climate resilience专题讨论四：不同地域、不同风险——因地制宜运用地球观测提升气候韧性

Climate change does not create the same risks everywhere. Island and coastal communities face rising seas and stronger storms, river basins face changing patterns of floods and droughts, and mountain regions face their own combination of environmental and climate-related hazards. Earth observation must therefore be adapted to different landscapes, institutions and development needs.气候变化在不同地区造成的风险并不相同。岛屿和沿海地区面临海平面上升和风暴加剧的威胁，大面积河流流域地区面临洪水与干旱模式变换的压力，山区则面临环境和气候因素叠加的复合风险。因此，地球观测必须适应不同地域、制度条件和发展需求。

Session overview会议简介

Bringing together experience from the Philippines, India, Lao PDR and the Kyrgyz Republic, this panel will examine how satellite observations and geospatial methods can support long-term climate-risk assessment and adaptation planning. The discussion will consider how countries can combine global satellite data with national and local information to identify vulnerable areas, monitor environmental change and prioritize resilience investments. The panel will focus on adapting Earth observation methods to different national and geographic contexts, rather than assuming that one technical solution can work everywhere.本场专题讨论汇集菲律宾、印度、老挝人民民主共和国和吉尔吉斯共和国的经验，探讨卫星观测和地理空间方法如何支持长期气候风险评估与适应规划。讨论将关注各国如何结合全球卫星数据与国家和地方信息，识别脆弱地区、监测环境变化并确定韧性投资优先事项，重点强调针对不同国家和地理环境调整地球观测方法，而不是假设一种技术方案可以适用于所有地区。

China has developed a broad Earth observation ecosystem that includes national satellite programmes, research institutions, regional cooperation mechanisms and specialized disaster applications. This panel examines how these capabilities have been applied in practice across different stages of disaster management.当前，中国已经形成较为完整的地球观测体系，其构成涵盖国家卫星发展顶层规划、体系化科研机构、区域合作机制和专业化应急管理应用。本场专题讨论将系统审视上述能力资源如何在灾害管理各个阶段得到实际应用。

Session overview会议简介

Drawing on examples from satellite operators, research institutions, regional organizations and development partnerships, panellists will discuss how observations are converted into services for monitoring hazards, assessing impacts and supporting recovery. The discussion will focus on operational results, remaining limitations and lessons that can be adapted in other countries. The central question will be: what makes an Earth observation capability genuinely useful in a disaster, and which elements of China’s experience can be transferred to other national and regional contexts?结合卫星运营机构、科研机构、区域组织和发展伙伴关系的案例，嘉宾将讨论如何把观测成果转化为灾害监测、影响评估和恢复支持服务。讨论将聚焦业务成效、现有限制以及可供其他国家借鉴的经验。核心问题是：哪些因素能使地球观测能力在灾害中真正发挥作用，中国经验中的哪些要素可应用于其他国家和区域环境？

Panel 8. Earth observation and disaster risk reduction in Asia and the Pacific: what works, what does not, and why专题讨论八：亚太地区地球观测与减轻灾害风险——哪些有效、哪些无效及其原因

This panel examines the final and often weakest part of the information chain: the point at which satellite analysis must be understood, trusted and acted upon by a disaster-management authority.地球观测产品即便在科学上准确、在传递上及时，也未必能够影响实际决策。本场专题讨论将聚焦信息链条中最后、往往也最为薄弱的一环，即卫星分析结果必须被灾害管理部门充分理解、赢得信任并付诸行动的转化环节。

Session overview会议简介

Practitioners from different national and institutional settings will discuss what happens after an Earth observation product reaches an agency. The conversation will address whether the information arrives in time, answers the questions decision-makers are actually asking, fits existing emergency procedures, and can be communicated clearly from technical analysts to operational leaders. It will also examine the effects of limited connectivity, unclear responsibilities and products that are too complex for immediate use. Rather than presenting successful technologies, the panel will identify practical failures in the operational workflow and define what an actionable Earth observation product should look like from the user’s perspective.来自不同国家和机构环境的实践者将讨论地球观测产品送达机构之后发生的情况，包括信息是否及时到达、是否回答决策者真正关心的问题、是否适配现有应急程序，以及技术分析人员能否向业务负责人清晰传达。讨论还将审视连通性不足、职责不清以及产品过于复杂而无法即时使用所造成的影响。本场讨论不会仅展示成功技术，而将识别业务流程中的实际失败，并从用户角度界定可行动的地球观测产品应具备哪些特征。
